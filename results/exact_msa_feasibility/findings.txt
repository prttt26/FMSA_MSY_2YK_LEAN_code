Exact MSA scalar closure recovery: completed kernel proof
Date: 2026-10-08

RESULT

The scalar one-component, one-Yukawa closure-recovery chain is complete.
exactMSA_hcore and exactMSA_kspace_residual are now theorems, not project
axioms. The original rational definitions, elimination, trigonometric
reduction, coefficient division, reassembly and Fourier bridges are all

The earlier global claim that this proof cannot be decomposed is superseded.

DOMAIN AND SCOPE

The unit-hard-core-diameter conventions are unchanged. The pure rational
residual theorem requires xi != 1, z != 0 and k != 0. The Fourier closure
requires z > 0, xi != 1 and k != 0. The factorization uses xi < 1, z > 0
and k != 0; its OZ-derived version additionally already assumes xi > 0.
K = 0 is included by a separate dictionary case, with no division-by-K
assumption in the final theorem. No division by G is required.

The old unrestricted hcore axiom was replaced with explicit regular physical
domain conditions. This is not a theorem about divergent z <= 0 tail
transforms, xi = 1 singular formulas, or the k = 0 limit. The existing OZ
consumer already supplied the new physical z > 0 condition.

This is a theorem at a Blum-Hoye root, not a proof that such a root exists
at every state. Mixture matrix closure axioms remain separate. No theorem
of universal finite-order thermodynamic validity, Maxwell coexistence,
strict finite-k stability or all-order branch topology is added.
Dt powers in the exact closure identity are Baxter-amplitude powers, not
orders N of the thermodynamic coupling-tau truncation.

CHECKED PROOF CHAIN

1. ExactMSAPhysicalDictionary and ExactMSADeltaDictionary identify actual
   bhF, bhP and the original closed k-space residual deltaActual with
   generated rational expressions at C=cos(k), S=sin(k), E=exp(-z), p=pi.
2. ExactMSAEliminationBridge proves
     denR * (Delta - m29*r29) = nRaw
   on explicitly stated nonzero-denominator conditions.
3. ExactMSAReductionBridge proves the polynomial identity
     nRaw = n1*Dt + n2*Dt^2 + mu*(S^2+C^2-1).
4. ExactMSASecondResidualBridge proves
     den33*r33 = r0 + Dt*r1.
   The existing sparse division certificates prove n1=q1*r0 and n2=q1*r1,
   and ExactMSASparseReassembly proves
     n1*Dt+n2*Dt^2 = Dt*q1*(r0+Dt*r1).
5. ExactMSAResidualRecovery substitutes the trigonometric identity and both
   root constraints, proves denR nonzero, and concludes deltaActual=0.
6. MSAFullFactorization rewrites the independently defined core and tail
   Fourier transforms, proves exactMSA_hcore, and derives the physical
   Baxter factorization. MSAFactorizationFromOZ supplies the two root
   constraints from the already proved OZ/Baxter primitive derivations.

A direct corollary exactMSA_structureDen_nonneg proves that the exact scalar
structure-factor denominator is nonnegative at every real nonzero k under
these root/domain conditions. Zeros are allowed. Strict stability and the
zero-wavevector limit require additional arguments.
