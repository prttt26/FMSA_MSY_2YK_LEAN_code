Exact MSA scalar closure-recovery proof: feasibility investigation
Date: 2026-10-08

RESULT

The historical direct-ring resource measurements do not establish that the
Exact MSA Lean proof is impossible or cannot be decomposed. A different proof
representation has now passed the actual polynomial-division checkpoints,
including both full nonzero amplitude coefficients, followed by a small
coefficient-reassembly theorem. The original physical hcore axiom is NOT yet
removed. This is positive feasibility evidence, not a completed end-to-end
proof of the physical closure.

SCOPE AND PROOF CHAIN

This experiment concerns the scalar, single-component, one-Yukawa closure.
It does not discharge the mixture matrix hcore axioms, finite-order physical
validity, coexistence, or finite-k stability.
The Dt powers below are coefficients in the exact Baxter-amplitude closure
identity, not orders N of the thermodynamic coupling-tau Taylor truncation.
Passing these checkpoints does not certify a high-order truncation's phase
diagram or resolve its possible nonphysical branches.

The algebraic prefix of msaexact6_scaletest.py in the project root reconstructs the
original scalar rational residual Delta and the two Blum-Hoye residuals r29
and r33. See checkpoint_metadata.json for the source path and SHA256.
With m29 = -z/(2*pi) * partial_K Delta, the source performs:

  R = Delta - m29*r29;
  R = nRaw / denR;
  nReduced = remainder of nRaw modulo S^2 + C^2 - 1;
  nReduced = n1*Dt + n2*Dt^2;
  numerator(r33) = r0 + r1*Dt;
  quotient = q1*Dt.

The source's symbolic polynomial division gives nReduced = quotient *
numerator(r33). The emitted Lean statements check the two coefficient
identities independently:

  n1 = q1*r0;
  n2 = q1*r1.

Their expression variables are [xi,z,k,G,C,S,Ez,p], where C,S,Ez,p will
eventually be identified with cos(k), sin(k), exp(-z), pi. In Lean they are
arbitrary real values, so these coefficient identities are not numerical
checks or statements restricted to sampled thermodynamic states.

ExactMSASparseReassembly.lean then proves for every real Dt:

  n1*Dt + n2*Dt^2 = Dt*q1*(r0 + Dt*r1).

It also proves that the left side vanishes when r0 + Dt*r1 = 0. Reassembly
treats q1,r0,r1 as three opaque real coefficient values; it does not repeat
a large expanded normalization.

WHAT IS ACTUALLY FORMAL

SparsePolynomialCertificate.lean defines a computable integer-coefficient
expression language and a sparse polynomial compiler. It proves that
compilation preserves evaluation in the reals. The compiler uses raw
products, a structurally recursive fuel-based merge sort, and adjacent
coefficient collection. Soundness does not depend on sorting completeness;
insufficient fuel can fail to certify an identity but cannot make a false
identity pass.

Each fixture keeps the right-side factor products unexpanded. Lean checks
that the difference compiles to the empty polynomial using decide +kernel,
then applies the compiler's proved semantic soundness. No native_decide,
new axiom, or admitted proof is used. The aggregate module contains five
axiom guards for the three checkpoint identities and two reassembly results.
Each depends only on propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound.

Important boundary: the mapping from the original rational MSA definitions
to these encoded coefficients, and the preceding clearing/reduction steps,
are still reconstructed and checked by SymPy outside Lean. The kernel proofs
certify the encoded identities, not that entire source-to-physics chain.

not on a small-memory laptop. Further coefficient grading may reduce it,
but that optimization has not been tested.

OLD BENCHMARK INTERPRETATION

The old msaexact6_scaletest.py emission calls expand on BOTH sides before
printing a Lean statement. These sides are identical canonical expressions
at all three sizes; that emitted equality could therefore be closed by rfl.
Its expensive ring run cannot establish an intrinsic proof-complexity lower
bound or certify the bridge from an unexpanded physical residual. The new
fixtures deliberately preserve the RHS factor products. We therefore do
not claim a controlled speedup ratio against that historical benchmark.

REMAINING SUBTASKS

1. Establish the Lean dictionary from msaA, msaQp, gam, bhF, bhP and the
   closed k-space transform formulas to the rational expression used here.
   Reuse ExactMSACertificate's existing Fourier-layer bridge; do not redo
   those radial integral proofs.

2. Audit regularity assumptions and clear denominators in Lean. For the
   physical regular domain, k != 0, z > 0, xi < 1, pi != 0, exp(-z) != 0
   supply ordinary nonvanishing facts. Terms such as (K*G)^2/K require a
   K = 0 case or a formal identity valid under Lean's totalized division.
   The existing axiom's broadly quantified domain must also be audited:
   a proof on the regular physical domain alone does not justify replacing
   an unrestricted statement at singular parameter values.

3. Certify the K-elimination identity and the numerator correspondence:
   Delta - m29*r29 = nRaw/denR. This can itself be divided into coefficient
   identities and handled with the same proved checker, but has not yet
   been generated and checked end to end.

4. Certify the trigonometric reduction as an explicit identity
   nRaw = nReduced + mu*(S^2 + C^2 - 1). Substitute sin/cos and apply
   the existing Pythagorean theorem, rather than normalizing the full
   polynomial again.

5. Identify numerator(r33) with the encoded r0 + Dt*r1, use h33 and
   nonvanishing denominators, apply full_division_zero_of_residual, then
   h29 and the Fourier bridge to discharge the physical hcore theorem.

The interfaces should be fixed before attempting these subtasks. Their
successful completion is plausible after the demonstrated division checks,
but their total cost and any singular-domain issues are still unmeasured.
For unequal-diameter mixtures the c-side piecewise dictionary and matrix
branch constraints add separate tasks; scalar success is not their proof.

FILES AND REPRODUCTION

From the project root:
  python Lean_code/scripts/msa_exact_sparse_feasibility.py

The source reconstruction took about 130 seconds in this experiment.
From Lean_code:
  lake build ExactMSASparseFeasibility
  lake build LeanCode

Final validation passed: ExactMSASparseFeasibility (8564 jobs), the default
LeanCode build (8816 jobs), Python syntax compilation, and git diff --check.
An isolated regeneration reproduced all five emitted Lean files: the three
fixtures and reassembly byte-for-byte, and the aggregate modulo whitespace.
The source SHA256 and all coefficient metadata also matched. See
regeneration_final_check.log and final_library_build.log. A first regeneration
comparison found only a reassembly doc-comment mismatch; the generator's
comment was aligned before the successful rerun. Earlier failed build and
prototype logs are historical attempts, not the final verification results.

The research library is deliberately outside defaultTargets and is not
imported by the production ExactMSA root while the physical bridge is open.
No paper TeX was edited by this investigation. The FMSA_paper thread has
been informed that the old global infeasibility conclusion must be narrowed,
while the paper's current physical-certification strength is unchanged.
