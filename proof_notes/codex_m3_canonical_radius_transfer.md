# M3 chain bound on the actual canonical branch

`M3TaylorRadius.radius_of_chainL` constructs a local analytic HS germ with vector formal-series radius at most 84 and non-summability for every real coupling of absolute value greater than 84. The returned germ is existential. `M3CanonicalRadiusTransfer.coeff_eq_branchCoefficients` proves its vector coefficient is the original `(D,G)` Taylor jet in every species/tail entry. `actual_canonical_root_coefficients_unique` then identifies that jet with the constructed maximal HS-seeded branch, using the actual MSA root and canonical interfaces near zero.

`maximal_branch_nonsummable_of_chainL` now gives non-summability of the original amplitude series on that actual branch for `|τ|>84`. `maximal_branch_radius_le_of_chainL` converts the same witness to the intrinsic `amplitudeRadius (branchCoefficients M.path) ≤ 84` using finite-component summability. At any separately certified canonical state `b>84`, `canonical_m3_state_beyond_chain_radius` gives an actual MSA root, canonicality, failure of summability of the original amplitude series at `b`, and arbitrarily late positive and negative coefficients in at least one original component. `exists_canonical_m3_state_with_nonsummable_series` constructs the maximal branch from the fixed M3 physical data; its real-prefix membership premise remains explicit.

The 4210-slab chain is intentionally external to the Lean package, and the real-prefix certificate is also external. Thus these declarations are a kernel-checked implication from the two numerical certificates, not an unconditional Lean instance of the M3 counterexample. No claim that every real root agrees with its Taylor series is made. All seven new theorem guards use only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`; foundation 1.0.17 is unchanged.
