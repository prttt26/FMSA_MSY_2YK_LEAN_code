# Arithmetic frequency mesh for mixture amplitude noescape

`MixtureFrequencyGridCoverage.lean` replaces the quantified frequency-window coverage premise by an explicit equally spaced finite mesh. For window half-width `L`, positive step `h`, and natural `n` satisfying `2L≤n h`, define `uniformSamples(L,h,n)={-L+j h : 0≤j≤n}`. `uniformSamples_cover` proves every `|k|≤L` lies less than one step to the right of a sample. The proof uses the natural floor of `(k+L)/h` and includes both window endpoints.

If a nonnegative global sample inverse budget is `Rbar` and the nonnegative Fourier moment budget is `Bmom`, the single strict inequality `Rbar h Bmom<1` makes every mesh slab zero-free. `uniformSamples_constant_cover` proves the previous continuum frequency coverage condition from this finite arithmetic check. `transformMomentBudget_nonneg` derives the required sign for the actual rational-exponential moment budget from the four nonnegative BH coefficient caps and a rate-domain `δ`.

`hs_uniform_mesh_amplitude_bound` composes that mesh argument with the actual same-HS-seed root-path noescape theorem. `hs_uniform_mesh_adjugate_amplitude_bound` replaces direct inverse bounds by a uniform positive determinant norm margin `m` and uniform adjugate entry-norm cap `Bbar` at each mesh sample; the inverse cap is `Bbar/m`. The resulting certificate contains four coefficient caps, `δ`, `h`, `n`, the two scalar mesh inequalities, and finitely many sampled determinant/adjugate bounds. The sampled bounds still quantify over **every coupling** on the proposed root prefix. A concrete interval continuation certificate must cover that continuum coupling parameter; a finite float scan does not do so.
